# Asset provenance

- `assets/hybrid-orbital-lab.html`: Orbital Observatory: How Orbitals Mix, version 1.4.0, reviewed on 2026-10-17. Preserves the normalized hydrogenic 2s/2p model and adds a separate normalized 3s/3p/3d model with idealized dsp2, sp3d, and sp3d2 sets, unused orbitals, and linked continuous s/p/d controls for one normalized orbital. Three simultaneous wavefunction, coherent-density, and s/p/d-group density-only-mixture panels share controls, cutoff, scale, and camera. The mixture removes cross-group interference while preserving within-group coherence. Analytic xy/xz/yz cross-sections share a coordinate probe with both densities and the three interference terms. Slice colors use fixed scales; numeric values are unclipped. The fixed reference sets remain unchanged. Chemical interpretation is explicitly qualified; the construction is not a molecular bonding calculation. Fixed-density surfaces use analytic edge roots and a center-refined n=3 mesh. Standalone SHA-256: `7693e137691030d2b2da3a2d790a3ebdfa31c05d9b35191e1f96d94335c4087f`. Three.js r170 and its MIT notice are embedded; the existing license and earlier displays are preserved.
- `assets/hybrid-orbital-lab-source.zip`: editable sources, portable display, reproducible standalone builder, independent mathematical and mesh checks, and scientific reviews for both basis models. SHA-256: `dc80c1fa9cb402a59c79d04ebfbb051bcb30cee74c779ecfad1aab0698c63fae`.
