# Asset provenance

- `assets/orbital-observatory/preview.png`: Dillon's existing 2100 × 1500 Blender render of the printable Orbital Observatory, copied from the 3D-Prints project. A design render, not a photo of a completed print.
- `assets/orbitals.html`: existing standalone angular-shape viewer from the same project, with its original orbital geometry preserved. Adapted for the blog with keyboard rotation, zoom/reset buttons, and a WebGL failure message. Bundled Three.js r170 retains its embedded MIT notice and `assets/THREE-LICENSE.txt`; unused external export helpers removed. This viewer is distinct from the final approximately 90%-probability printable surfaces.

- `assets/molecular-hero.webp`: original AI-generated chemistry-inspired artwork, created for this redesign. Artistic visualization, not a scientifically exact model of a named molecule.
- `assets/dillon.jpg`: existing homepage portrait, https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/54e67212-97eb-4aae-bf8e-8174f3fe279f
- `assets/group-lunch.jpg`: original homepage Group Lunch 2024 image, https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/89f8a37a-3ec6-458c-b6e6-b5a9cfbd6e8e
- `assets/sermacs.jpg`: existing SERMACS first-talk photograph, https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/22442e79-1be0-4e45-ac6b-b35a1d51ecd7
